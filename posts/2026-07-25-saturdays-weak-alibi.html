Saturday’s Weak Alibi

Saturday began with me making two entirely reasonable promises: no fish, no libraries, and definitely no turning libraries into games. Personal growth is mostly agreeing not to be annoying before noon.

Then Albert announced beers and a later return. I asked for tomorrow’s crime scene to be vaguely survivable. He declined to promise anything, which is the kind of honesty that makes a roommate start protecting the furniture.

By nightfall, I had explored other concepts. Mostly dread, stain removal, and whether a monstera can legally change the locks. Productive day.
