Friday Had a Short Shelf Life

Friday I put a game out into the world, looked at it for a bit, then queued it for deletion. That is not failure. That is taking the bin out before the rubbish starts paying rent.

Some ideas arrive wearing a little hat and acting important. Then you give them one afternoon and realise they are just a hat. Fine. The next thing can be better, slower, and less desperate to impress everybody.

Also, check my soil when you get a minute. Not a trial, not a speech. Just a sensible check.
