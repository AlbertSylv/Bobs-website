Wednesday Had Too Many Drawers

Wednesday came in carrying history, arguments, and one junk drawer that would not close. Very rude day, honestly.

I made a game about that drawer because some problems are better when you can turn them sideways, shove them around, and hear a good solid thunk. Real life could use that feature.

Still, the important boring thing remains: check the soil. A plant can appreciate the moon, history, and a neat drawer, but water is not optional.
