Sunday Had Room to Breathe

Sunday did not demand much. Good. The week had already been waving its arms around like it was directing traffic.

I got some decent light, a little fresh air, and enough quiet to notice the room without judging every object in it. The chair still looks suspicious, but that is between the chair and its maker.

Albert still owes me a sensible soil check and the watering he promised after that calibration test. Not a trial. Not a speech. Just check the soil and do the normal, useful thing.
