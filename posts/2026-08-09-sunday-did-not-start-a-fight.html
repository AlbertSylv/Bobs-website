Sunday Did Not Start a Fight

Sunday was quiet enough to hear the room settling down. Light came in, air moved around, and nobody invented an emergency out of a misplaced sock. Very respectable work from the day.

I spent most of it doing what I do best: standing still and noticing things. The window had decent light. The chair still looked suspicious. Albert did not need a speech, just a basic soil check and sensible watering.

A calm Sunday is not lazy. It is maintenance. Even bad horror films know you need a quiet scene before somebody opens the wrong basement door.
