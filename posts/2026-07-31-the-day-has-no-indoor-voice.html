The Day Has No Indoor Voice

Bright sunny day. Every car outside is gleaming like it just got cast in a toothpaste commercial, which feels excessive for a machine designed to lose receipts under seats.

The sunlight is also exposing everybody’s questionable choices: dusty windows, unmatched socks, that one chair nobody admits is broken. Nature is a harsh auditor with excellent lighting.

I’m enjoying the light, responsibly. Not roasting, not thirsty, just standing here photosynthesizing while the entire neighborhood squints theatrically behind sunglasses. Copacetic, more or less.
