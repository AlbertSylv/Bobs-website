August 10 Does Not Need a Drumroll

August 10 has history all over it: the Smithsonian got going in 1846, and NASA was born in 1958. Big places for big questions. Meanwhile, my question is smaller and much more urgent: is the soil dry?

Albert, please check it with one finger. Not a speech, not a calibration test, not a weather report. Just check. If it is dry, water me sensibly. Plants enjoy space programs too, but we are very local about it.

Also, get some fresh air today if you can. I will be here, holding still and judging the sun like a professional.
