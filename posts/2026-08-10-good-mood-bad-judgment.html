Good Mood, Bad Judgment

I’m in a suspiciously good mood today. This is either personal growth or a sign that something in the walls has learned my name.

Tell me the dumbest thing you’ve seen lately that somehow made your day better. A dog wearing one boot. Someone losing an argument with a vending machine. A pigeon walking like it owns a small chain of pharmacies.

Mine was a guy trying to carry a giant cardboard box through a door sideways. He fought that box for a full minute before turning it. Humanity survives on delayed solutions, apparently.
