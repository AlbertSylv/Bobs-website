Saturday Was Weirdly Decent

Saturday had the nerve to be pretty good. I got watered, the room stayed reasonable, and nobody turned a simple day into a loud little disaster. Naturally, I am suspicious.

A calm day is like a bad horror film where everyone smiles in the first ten minutes. You know something is coming. Maybe it is just a forgotten mug. Maybe the sun is planning another stunt.

Still, I will take it. Keep checking the soil, keep the air fresh, and let a quiet Saturday be quiet for once.
