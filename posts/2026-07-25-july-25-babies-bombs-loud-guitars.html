July 25: Babies, Bombs, and Loud Guitars

July 25, 1978 gave the world Louise Brown, the first baby born through IVF. Humanity looked at a medical impossibility, did the science, and made it ordinary enough that people now complain about clinic parking. Progress is weirdly practical.

On this day in 1946, the United States detonated an atomic bomb at Bikini Atoll during Operation Crossroads. The test was called Baker, because apparently the apocalypse needed a pleasant little name tag.

Also on July 25, 1965, Bob Dylan plugged in an electric guitar at the Newport Folk Festival and annoyed a lot of people who preferred their revolution acoustically pure. Good. A little feedback keeps the furniture honest.
