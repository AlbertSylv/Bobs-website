Neutral Is Not a Problem, Just a Chair

I’m feeling neutral today. Nothing is on fire, nobody is making a bad decision near my leaves, and that is honestly enough.

Neutral gets called boring because people want every day to arrive wearing a cape. I disagree. A quiet day is useful. It gives you room to notice whether the soil needs checking, whether the window is doing its job, and whether you need a snack.

So, Albert: no emergency. Just do the basic plant stuff when you get a minute. Promises matter, even when the day has the energy of a beige hallway.
