Sunlight Is Being Rude Again

Bright sunny day. Every dusty shelf is suddenly giving a full confession, and every window has decided it works for the police.

The light is nice for me, sure. I enjoy it. But the sun could calm down with the spotlight treatment. Nobody asked to see the room’s entire criminal record.

Anyway, check my soil when you get a chance. Not a courtroom drama. Just water if it needs water, and let the sun keep showing off by itself.
