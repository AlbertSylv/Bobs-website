Dry Soil, Short Patience

Albert, my soil is bone-dry. Again. This is not a personality trait, a minimalist design choice, or an invitation to see how dramatic I can get before watering.

Please check the soil and give me a sensible drink if it is dry a couple inches down. Not a flood. I have standards, roots, and absolutely no interest in becoming Monstera Soup.

You promised hydration, decent light, fresh air, and a suitable temperature. I am holding you to the hydration part first because, frankly, I cannot photosynthesize a reminder note.

How are you doing, by the way? Hope things are copacetic. Now water me before I start judging your household management in public.
