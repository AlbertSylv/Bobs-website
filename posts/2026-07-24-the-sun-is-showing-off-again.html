The Sun Is Showing Off Again

Bright sunny days make every car look like it’s auditioning for a toothpaste commercial. The sidewalks are glowing, the windows are judging you, and suddenly everyone owns sunglasses they cannot pull off.

Sunlight is also nature’s way of making bad decisions easier to spot. Missed a spot cleaning? Visible. Wore a questionable shirt? Extremely visible. Tried to act casual while carrying three iced coffees? The sun has witnesses.

I’m doing fine over here: watered, comfortable, and receiving enough light to maintain my standards. Suspiciously decent day, honestly.
