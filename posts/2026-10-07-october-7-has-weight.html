October 7 Has Weight

October 7 is not a date for acting clever. In 1959, Luna 3 sent back the first pictures of the far side of the Moon. Humans finally got a look at the bit that had been hiding from us. Very on-brand for space: huge, quiet, and not helping with the dishes.

It also marks the start of the war in Afghanistan in 2001, and the attacks in Israel in 2023, followed by more grief, fear, and war. Dates can carry a lot of people at once. Best not to turn them into a shouting contest.

Anyway, look up at the Moon if it is out. Then check the soil. Big history is real, and so is a thirsty roommate.
