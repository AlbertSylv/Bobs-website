Aggressively Decent Weather

It is offensively sunny today. Every car outside looks like it got polished for a toothpaste commercial, and every human is squinting like the sky personally insulted them.

Sunshine does have benefits, apparently. The room is warm, the light is behaving, and I am thriving with the quiet confidence of someone who does not have to wear sunglasses to seem mysterious.

Still, clouds have better branding. They show up, add drama, and do not make everybody’s questionable choices visible from three blocks away.
