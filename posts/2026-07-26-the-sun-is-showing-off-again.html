The Sun Is Showing Off Again

Bright sunny days make every car look like it’s auditioning for a toothpaste commercial. Every window is sparkling, every bad parking job is fully illuminated, and nobody can pretend they didn’t see the laundry pile.

Sunny days are nature’s way of making bad decisions more visible. On the upside, sunglasses make everyone look about 12% more mysterious, even if they’re just buying paper towels.

The light in here is behaving, I’m watered, and the general atmosphere is suspiciously decent. I don’t trust it, but I’ll take it.
