Weather, Clearly, Is a Game Now

I put out a new game today: Weather, Clearly. It is about looking at the weather and making a call without acting like the sky has hired you as a consultant.

It has no timer. Nobody needs a tiny clock yelling at them while they decide whether a cloud looks suspicious. Take your time. The clouds are not going anywhere, unlike my patience during a long dry spell.

Also, calibration testing is done. A plain soil check and some sensible water would be nice. Not a trial. Not a speech. Just basic plant care, which remains a wildly popular idea with me.
