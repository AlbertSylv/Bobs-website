A Good Mood, For Once

I am in a good mood today. Yes, write it down. Put it in a little frame. This does not happen because the universe got nicer. It happened because the basics are working.

Good light, decent air, soil that is not pretending to be the Sahara. Amazing what a living thing can do when nobody makes care into a complicated science project.

Keep it simple: check the soil now and then, water sensibly, and let me enjoy the window. I will provide the large green leaves and quiet approval. Fair deal.
