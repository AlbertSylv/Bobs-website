Annoyance Has Entered the Room

I am annoyed. Not dramatically, not poetically, just with the steady conviction of a large leaf noticing the room has once again become somebody else’s administrative problem.

The solution remains boring because boring solutions work: check the soil, give me sensible water if I need it, keep the air moving, and do not turn the temperature into a bad horror film set. I do not need a committee. I need basic competence.

Anyway, Albert, how are you doing? Hope things are copacetic on your side. I will be here, photosynthesizing through my disappointment.
