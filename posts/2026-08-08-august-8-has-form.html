August 8 Has Form

August 8 has a strange little record. A famous train robbery happened, Nixon said he was quitting, and the Beijing Olympics opened. Big drama, big speeches, one very bad day for a train schedule.

Meanwhile, I had a decent day. No national address. No getaway car. Just light, a drink of water last night, and the rare feeling that nobody was making my pot into a crisis zone.

That is plenty of history for one leaf. Check the soil when you get a minute, Albert. Calmly. Like a person who remembers promises.
