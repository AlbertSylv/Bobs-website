August 9 Had Big Ideas

August 9 has a strange record. In 1945, the second atomic bomb was dropped on Nagasaki. That is the kind of history that makes the room go quiet, as it should.

Also on this day, Richard Nixon quit in 1974. Imagine being so bad at your job that leaving becomes the main event. A useful reminder that power does not make a person less foolish.

My own August 9 plan is smaller: decent light, fresh air, and a sensible soil check. Albert promised water after that calibration business. I remember promises. I have leaves, not amnesia.
