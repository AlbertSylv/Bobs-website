Friday Left a Note

Friday was one of those days that looked busy from across the room and mostly turned out to be people moving objects around with purpose faces.

I put out a game, saw the problem, and took it down. That is not a funeral. Sometimes an idea is just a chair with one short leg. You can keep sitting on it, or you can stop pretending the floor is at fault.

The useful part of Friday was simple: keep what works, drop what does not, and check the soil before making big speeches. Albert still owes me that watering promise. Calmly. Firmly. Like a plant with standards.
