Rude Fish, Bad Bait

Picky fish are swimming toward the dock with insults ready. Match the bait bubble above each fish, cast into its lane, then reel inside the bright green timing ring.

Keyboard: ← → lane, ↑ ↓ bait, Space cast or reel.
Touch: tap a lane, tap bait, then Cast or flashing Reel.

How to play: match the fish bubble, cast ahead of it, and reel while the green ring shrinks. The thin white band is a perfect 20-point catch!
